Your privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Welcome Home Cleaning Co. ("we", "us") is a cleaning business based in Milton Keynes. This page explains what personal information we collect, why we use it, and the rights you have. We only collect what we need to quote for and carry out your clean.

Who we are and how to contact us

We are the "controller" of your information, which means we decide how it is used. You can contact us about anything on this page by calling 07808 341760 or by messaging us on Instagram at @welcomehomecleaningc0.

What information we collect

  • When you ask for a quote: your name, phone number, postcode, the service you want, the number of bedrooms and bathrooms, and any notes you choose to add.
  • When we book and carry out a clean: the address of the property, how we get in (for example key or access arrangements), details about the property, your booking dates and times, and payment records.
  • When you contact us: your phone number and the messages, calls, reviews or social media messages you send us.

We do not ask for, or want, sensitive information such as health details. Please do not include it in your messages.

How our quote form works

This website does not store what you type into the quote form. When you press the button, it opens WhatsApp (or your email app, if we offer it) with your details already filled in, and nothing is sent until you press send yourself. Once you send it, your message is handled by that messaging service as well as by us.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently use cookies or visitor tracking. If we add them in future, we will update this page and ask for your consent where the law requires it. Links to Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Why we use your information

  • To give you a quote, book and carry out your clean. We need to do this to provide our service to you (the legal reason is "contract", which includes steps you ask us to take before a booking).
  • To run our business sensibly. For example, replying to enquiries, keeping records of work done and handling complaints (the legal reason is our "legitimate interests").
  • To meet legal duties. For example, keeping accounting and tax records (the legal reason is "legal obligation").

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it to make automated decisions about you.

Who we share it with

We only share your information when we need to, and only the parts that are needed:

  • Members of our team who carry out your clean.
  • Services we use to run the business, such as messaging, payment and accounting tools.
  • Authorities or advisers, where the law requires it or to protect our legal rights.

How long we keep it

  • Quote enquiries that do not lead to a booking: usually no longer than 12 months.
  • Customer and booking records: while you are a customer and for a reasonable time afterwards in case of questions or complaints.
  • Accounting and payment records: for as long as the law requires, which is usually at least 6 years.
  • Key and access details: only for as long as we need them to carry out your clean.

How we keep it safe

We take sensible steps to protect your information, such as limiting who can see it and treating keys, access codes and addresses with care.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information, in some circumstances;
  • ask us to restrict how we use it, or object to how we use it, in some circumstances;
  • ask for the information you gave us in a format you can reuse (this is called "portability").

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details above. We aim to reply within one month.

Making a complaint

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, on 0303 123 1113 or at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Children

Our services are for adults and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date at the top.
